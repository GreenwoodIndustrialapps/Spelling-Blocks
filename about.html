About Spelling Blocks

A spelling game made with care.

Hello

Spelling Blocks is made by me, Steve Greenwood. I wanted a spelling game that is fun to play and that anyone of any age can pick up: calm, colourful and honest. No adverts, no accounts, and nothing collected about the person playing.

Every word is played in two small steps. First you pop the letters that do not belong. Then you build the word, one block at a time, and earn a star for each part. When you are ready for a bigger test, Challenge adds a stopwatch.

Where the words come from

The words for each age are drawn from published spelling lists for English primary schools, and the game gets harder as the ages go up.
