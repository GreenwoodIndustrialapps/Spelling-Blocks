Privacy Support

Privacy policy

Spelling Blocks collects nothing about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, receive or share any information about you or your child.

What stays on the phone

The app remembers a few things on the phone itself so the game works: the last words shown, how recent words went for each age, and whether sound is on. This never leaves the phone. Deleting the app removes it.

Voice

The spoken help on the grown-ups page uses the voice built into your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

The unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details or your Apple account.

Children

The app is made for children and needs no personal information. It has no links to other websites or apps.

Word lists

The words for each age are drawn from published spelling lists for English primary schools. Adapted from English Appendix 1: Spelling, Department for Education, 2013. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Changes

If this ever changes, the new version will be in the app and on our website.
