Common questions

What ages is it for?

Reading ages 5 to 11. There are four reading age levels: 5 to 6, 6 to 7, 7 to 9 and 9 to 11. The 5 to 6 level is free.

What does the unlock include?

One purchase, made once in the App Store, opens the 6 to 7, 7 to 9 and 9 to 11 levels and Challenge. There is no subscription and nothing else to buy.

How do I buy it, or get it back on a new phone?

Both are on the grown-ups page, which asks a short sum before it opens so purchases are never made by accident. To get your purchase back, tap Restore purchase on that page, signed in with the same Apple account.

Does it need the internet?

No. Only buying or restoring the unlock needs a connection.

What is saved on the phone?

The last words shown, how recent words went for each age, and whether sound is on. It stays on the phone, and deleting the app removes it. Read the privacy policy.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button on the home page.

What is Challenge?

A timed round for confident spellers. Tap the wrong letters in order, from a to z, then spell the word before the time runs out.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhones with iOS 16.6 or later. It also runs on an iPad, at phone size.
