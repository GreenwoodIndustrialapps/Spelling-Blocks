Learn to spell by playing

Pop the letters that do not belong, then spell the word. A friendly spelling game with levels for reading ages 5 to 11.

How it works

Every word has two parts, and a star is won for each one.

First, pop the wrong lettersTap the blocks that do not belong in the word.
Now spell the wordTap the right blocks in order and watch the word build up.
